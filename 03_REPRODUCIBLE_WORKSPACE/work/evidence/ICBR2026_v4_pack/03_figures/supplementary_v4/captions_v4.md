# Figure captions (v4 restyle)

Figures are re-drawn by `../render_supp_v4.py` from the frozen tables in `05_data_and_code`; no value, interval or count differs from the v3 figures. Colours follow the paper: ultramarine and circles for single-response objectives, vermilion and squares for three-response objectives, grey for references.

**Fig. 1 | Connectivity, limited recordings and a held-out response in one simulated network.** **a,** Known signed connectivity J for the first frozen network, J5000 (blue positive, orange negative weights; symmetric colour limits ±0.42 around exact zero); columns are presynaptic and rows postsynaptic nodes. Filled markers beside the matrix identify the recorded nodes N6, N8 and N11, the open vermilion marker the unrecorded readout N4, and the open plum marker the stimulated node N0. **b,** Actual noisy calibration observations at the three recorded nodes in two trials, each reset to the same known initial state (Gaussian observation noise SD 0.05, stride 4). Trial 2 is shifted by 6 model-time units for display; the dashed line marks the trial boundary, not continuous evolution, and lines connect observations only within a trial. Vertical scales differ between nodes. **c,** Stimulated minus matched zero-input control rate at N4 (displayed in units of 10⁻⁴); the upper strip shows the stimulus input at N0 during model time 1 to 2. The integrated response q = 2.43518 × 10⁻⁴ (a.u. × model time) is Δt times the sum of the 120 frozen-model differences (Δt = 0.05), not a new trapezoidal integral. The case is the fixed first-index instance (broad uncertainty, truth and noise draw 0, target 0, endpoints excluded, M = 3, nominal single-response selection), chosen before its effect was inspected. Independent n = 1 network; no interval is shown and no between-method effect or biological validity is claimed. All quantities are in dimensionless model units.

**Supplementary Fig. 1 | Complete paired primary differences.** Each panel shows all 40 independent network-level ensemble-minus-nominal raw-MSE differences for the single-response (a) and three-response (b) comparison, including exact ties and the influential networks; the step curve is the empirical distribution function and every sorted value is drawn as a marker. The horizontal axis is in units of 10⁻⁵ q²; negative values favour the ensemble. Extreme values (−16.2 and +6.3 in a; −1.2 and +6.3 in b) are labelled, not truncated. Exactly 13 (a) and 26 (b) of the 40 differences are zero. The distributions are descriptive; no interval is drawn, and the primary intervals remain those in Table 1 and Fig. 2. No equivalence margin was specified, and nothing here shows that the two selection rules perform alike.

**Supplementary Fig. 2 | All recording conditions under matched uncertainty.** Panels a–d show all-site/M = 3, endpoint-excluded/M = 3, all-site/M = 6 and endpoint-excluded/M = 6. For each of six policies (nominal and ensemble single-response, nominal and ensemble three-response, activity, random) every one of the 40 independent networks is shown as one point, with truth, noise and readout repeats averaged within network. Thick black bars are the aggregate means and thin black lines the exploratory pointwise 95% network-cluster intervals from the frozen summary table. Open markers denote nominal and filled markers ensemble selection; horizontal jitter only separates overlapping points. All panels share the logarithmic response-MSE axis (10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻²). No point or tail is removed. Intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted and no pairwise or equivalence claim is made.

**Supplementary Fig. 3 | All recording conditions under broad uncertainty.** As Supplementary Fig. 2 for the broad prior: panels a–d show all-site/M = 3, endpoint-excluded/M = 3, all-site/M = 6 and endpoint-excluded/M = 6; 40 independent networks per policy and panel; black bars and lines are aggregate means and exploratory pointwise 95% network-cluster intervals; shared logarithmic axes; open nominal, filled ensemble; jitter is horizontal only. The primary comparison in Fig. 2 corresponds to panel b. No method-specific tail is removed, and the panels do not support any claim of equivalence or superiority.

**Supplementary Fig. 4 | All recording conditions under hidden bias shift.** As Supplementary Fig. 2 for the hidden-bias-shift condition: panels a–d show all-site/M = 3, endpoint-excluded/M = 3, all-site/M = 6 and endpoint-excluded/M = 6; 40 independent networks per policy and panel; black bars and lines are aggregate means and exploratory pointwise 95% network-cluster intervals; shared logarithmic axes; open nominal, filled ensemble; jitter is horizontal only. No data are filtered, and the intervals are descriptive and not multiplicity-adjusted.

**Supplementary Fig. 5 | Nondegenerate linear-Gaussian positive control.** All 144 target/design evaluations from 12 independent linear systems compare the analytic MAP MSE (horizontal) with the Monte Carlo MSE from 8,192 draws per system (vertical); the dashed identity line is the analytic reference. The independent unit is the system (n = 12); the 144 points are evaluations within systems, not independent neural networks, and no confidence intervals are displayed. Agreement validates this computational special case only; it does not validate biological assumptions.
