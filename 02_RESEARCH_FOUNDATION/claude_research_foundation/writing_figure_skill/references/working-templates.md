# Planning tables to reuse selectively

## Source card

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Source / version / URL | Stable identifier and access date |
| Access depth | Full main text, selected sections, figures/legends, abstract or excerpt |
| Closest scientific question | A precise question rather than a topic keyword |
| Evidence design | Unit, intervention, comparison, endpoint, validation split |
| Concrete craft example | Paragraph location or figure/panel and what it does |
| Adopt / adapt / reject | The useful move and conditions for transfer |
| Citation role in our study | Scientific support, method provenance, limitation, or presentation-only |

## Paragraph plan

| ID | Question inherited | One intended inference | Evidence / citation role | What the next paragraph must resolve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | Why care about this use? | Specify the scientific decision | Direct motivation / closest study | Which uncertainty obstructs it? |

Use as many or as few rows as the actual argument needs. Do not insert a paragraph solely to fill a template.

## Claim-to-evidence ledger

| Claim | Evidence location | Scope / uncertainty | Alternative explanation | Permissible wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The ensemble improves prediction | Prespecified paired comparison | Model, target, pool, interval | Task specialisation, estimator/solver differences | Decide from actual estimate and interval; do not assume success |

## Figure contract

| Panel | Reader's question | Unit / pairing | Exact columns and transformation | Summary / interval | Full-range and visual checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Do the methods differ on the same systems? | One pair per independent system | Source columns, raw/log scale | Identity line or paired effect | Shared limits, all instances retained |

## Delivery check

Are the claims and uncertainty the same in title, abstract, figures and Discussion? Do citations support their adjacent statements? Can a reader reconstruct the independent n, repeated measurements and interval? Is each visual readable at final size? Are source data and code identifiable? Which scientific questions remain unanswered after the editorial improvements?
