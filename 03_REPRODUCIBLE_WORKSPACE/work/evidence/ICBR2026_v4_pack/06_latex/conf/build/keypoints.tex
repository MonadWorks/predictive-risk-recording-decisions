\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Decisions.} The nominal and prior-averaged criteria chose identical recordings in 68\% (82/120) of single-target designs and 65\% (26/40) of shared designs, so a gain can arise only in the remaining designs.
\item
  \textbf{Error and forecast.} The two frozen primary comparisons and the primary forecast comparison all have intervals that include zero. In this simulated setting the shared-objective interval does not extend below a reduction of about 3\% (approximate, heavy-tailed data); the single-response interval is too wide to exclude either a gain or a loss. The forecast mean is driven by one network.
\item
  \textbf{Conditional signal.} In an exploratory 100-unit family (n = 8 networks, no multiplicity adjustment) four of eight settings favoured prior averaging with intervals excluding zero and one reversed in sign: a hypothesis for a confirmatory study, not a result.
\item
  \textbf{What transfers, and limits.} A three-step audit (decision agreement, response error, forecast accuracy), numerical controls and a proposed confirmatory test. Simulation only, with known connectivity and a correct observation model.
\end{itemize}
